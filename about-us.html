Our Background

ALMINGO S.L. was founded in 2010 as a company specialized in enterprise software development and technology consulting. Headquartered in Bilbao, we have established ourselves as a reference provider for companies in the industrial, financial and services sectors in the Basque Country and nationwide.

We have a portfolio of more than 30 corporate clients and have successfully completed over 50 digital transformation projects, custom application development and legacy system modernization. Our working methodology is based on ISO 9001:2015 standards, guaranteeing maximum quality in all our deliverables.

In 2024, we initiated a commercial expansion process towards international markets, maintaining our commitment to technical excellence, information security and GDPR regulatory compliance.
