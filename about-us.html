About ALMINGO S.L.

Innovation, quality and commitment since 2010

Our History

ALMINGO S.L. was founded in 2010 with a clear vision: to democratize access to high-quality technology solutions for businesses of all sizes. From our humble beginnings in Bilbao, we have grown into a benchmark for software development in the Basque Country.

Over the years, we have worked with more than 30 clients, completed over 50 successful projects, and most importantly, built long-lasting relationships based on trust and excellence.

In 2024, under new management, we began an exciting phase of international expansion while maintaining our commitment to sustainability and technological innovation.
