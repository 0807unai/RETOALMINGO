Our New Project: The Almingo Game

We believe in learning through play. Our latest project, **Almingo**, is a multiplayer bingo game designed to promote and validate knowledge about **Sustainability**. It's a fun, collective experience for any team looking to build awareness on crucial environmental and social topics.

This gamified solution helps companies meet their training goals in an engaging way, fully aligned with our new management's focus on innovation.
